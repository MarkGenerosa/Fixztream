FixZtream

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

In short: FixZtream collects no personal data, sends nothing over the internet, and has no analytics or tracking. Everything it records stays on your own computer.

What FixZtream does

FixZtream is a browser extension that runs a fixed set of Windows troubleshooting tasks — restarting the Windows Update services, resetting the Windows Update cache, and installing Chinese language components — through a companion helper program installed on your PC.

Information stored on your device

To show you an activity log, the extension keeps the following in your browser's local extension storage:

  • A record of each task you run: its name, start and end time, result, and the text output of the task.
  • Your chosen language components (for example, Handwriting or OCR).
  • The most recent status of the Windows Update and BITS services and the size of the update cache folder.

The companion helper also appends a one-line summary of each task to %ProgramData%\FixZtream\activity.log on your computer. Task output may include your Windows user name and computer name.

This information never leaves your device. You can clear the extension's log at any time with the Clear button, and remove the file log by running the helper's uninstaller with -RemoveLogs.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data.
  • We do not use analytics, cookies, advertising or third-party services.
  • The extension does not read your browsing history, the contents of web pages, or your tabs.

Network activity

The extension itself makes no network requests. When you choose to install a language, Windows downloads the components from Microsoft's Windows Update service (or your organisation's update server), exactly as if you had installed them from Windows Settings. That download is governed by Microsoft's privacy terms.

Permissions

  • Native messaging — to send the selected task to the helper on your PC and receive its output.
  • Storage — to keep the activity log and your settings on your device.
  • Notifications — to tell you when a task finishes.
  • Side panel — to let you keep the tool open beside your pages.
